About Flipkart

Flipkart, founded in 2007 by Sachin and Binny Bansal, is one of India's leading e-commerce platforms. Originally started as an online bookstore, it quickly diversified its offerings to include a wide range of products such as electronics, fashion, home appliances, and groceries. Headquartered in Bengaluru, Flipkart has played a pivotal role in shaping India's e-commerce landscape.


One of Flipkart's key strategies has been its focus on understanding and catering to the unique needs of Indian consumers. This includes offering cash-on-delivery payment options, easy returns, and delivery services to even the remotest parts of the country.
